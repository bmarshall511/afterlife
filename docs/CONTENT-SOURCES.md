# Content sources (games v2)

Every value in **The Line** (`src/content/data/line.json`) and **Closest Without Going Over** (`src/content/data/closest.json`) carries `source {title, url, verification}`. All text is our own paraphrase. Checked **2026-10-27**.

Method note: several authoritative sites (britannica.com, whc.unesco.org, inegi.org.mx, metro.cdmx.gob.mx) block direct fetching; for those, values were confirmed from domain-restricted search results that quote the official page, and the `verification` text says so. Nothing relies on blogs, news-only pages or Wikipedia.

## Dropped as unverifiable

- Line: Mexican city populations (INEGI ranking page not fetchable), state areas (only Chihuahua confirmed), animal speeds (zoo pages give tied/ranged figures), Rivera/Kahlo artwork dates, Bell telephone patent date (single item), Río Bravo length (blank in SEMARNAT table), Metropolitan Cathedral start date, Velcro, Post-it, Tabasco, Hershey bar, Doritos dates; lion weight (tie).
- Closest: Posada birth year (Britannica 1851 vs LoC 1852), Popocatépetl height (INEGI publications disagree), Mariana Trench depth, Grand Canyon length (NPS pages disagree), Frida Kahlo self-portrait count.
- Lotería: escalera = poker straight in Spanish (RAE returned 403), Hidalgo bell/15 September ritual, Yaqui deer dance, siren etymology — riddles rewritten without those claims.
